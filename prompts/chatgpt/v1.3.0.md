Use `promptVersion = 1.3.0` in the generated status object and immutable snapshot.

## Rich hourly forecast addition

Keep every existing `forecast[]` field and every existing 24-slot timestamp, score, dominant-mode, action, and status rule unchanged. Add these three fields to every one of the 24 hourly forecast entries:

```json
{
  "analysis": "A concise explanation of this hour's published scores and conditional risk path.",
  "drivers": ["A specific evidence-based driver or conditional checkpoint"],
  "news": [
    {
      "title": "Title of an already-published, verified item",
      "url": "https://example.com/the-verified-item",
      "source": "Optional publisher name"
    }
  ]
}
```

The fields have exact bounds:

- `analysis` is a trimmed, nonempty string of at most 360 characters. Keep it concise and distinguish facts already observed by `generatedAt` from conditional future paths.
- `drivers` contains 1-5 unique, trimmed, nonempty strings. Each driver is at most 120 characters.
- `news` contains 0-3 items. Use an empty array when no relevant already-published news belongs to that hour.
- Each news item contains only `title`, `url`, and optional `source`.
- `title` is trimmed and nonempty, at most 180 characters.
- `url` is a verified public HTTP(S) URL, at most 2048 characters.
- Optional `source` is a trimmed, nonempty publisher name, at most 80 characters.

## News and future-knowledge boundary

Hourly `news` is evidence known at the assessment cutoff, not a prediction of what will be reported during a future forecast hour.

- Include only material items already published and verified no later than `generatedAt`.
- Never invent a future headline, article, announcement, source, URL, release outcome, earnings result, policy decision, geopolitical development, or market reaction.
- Never present a scheduled future event as news. Keep scheduled macro, central-bank, earnings, and other calendar items in the inherited top-level `events` array and, where relevant, describe the known checkpoint in `drivers`.
- A scheduled release that has already occurred by `generatedAt` may appear in `news` only when its actual outcome and linked reporting have been verified.
- Forecast analysis may describe conditional scenarios, but it must not imply that a conditional future event or price path has already happened.
- Do not add filler news merely to avoid an empty array.
- Do not place private calibration, scheduler notes, credentials, tokens, cookies, broker/VPS/account details, balances, position sizes, prop-firm information, private strategy code, or private trading history anywhere in an hourly forecast field or nested object. The inherited public-repository safety rules remain absolute.

Only the current clock-hour slot may describe genuinely observed price-path structure as a current fact. Every later slot must frame unobserved price-path structure conditionally, for example as a checkpoint, assumption, invalidation, or scenario. Do not carry a current observation into later hours as though it were already observed there.

The same verified item or persistent driver may support more than one forecast hour when genuinely relevant, but each hour's analysis and driver selection must explain that specific hour. Do not publish 24 copies or near-copies of generic boilerplate. Repetition is acceptable only for a genuinely persistent named driver, while the concise analysis must still state its hour-specific relevance or conditional change.

For the inherited first six `forecastDetail[]` entries, keep each concise `comment` semantically consistent with the corresponding enriched `forecast[].analysis`. The comment may add useful detail, but it must not contradict that hour's analysis, drivers, scores, mode, action, or status. All inherited mechanical timestamp and score consistency checks remain unchanged.

Before the one permitted snapshot write, validate the complete candidate against `schemas/risk-state.schema.json`, `scripts/lib.mjs`, and the inherited exact time-series contract. A v1.3.0 candidate is invalid if even one forecast slot is missing or violates `analysis`, `drivers`, or `news`.

The inherited snapshot-only publication rule is unchanged. Publish exactly one new immutable snapshot under `providers/chatgpt/snapshots/**` and stop. The deterministic finalizer copies the complete snapshot to `status.json`, preserving the rich hourly forecast, while `signal.json` and `history.json` remain compact derived publications and do not copy hourly rich content.
