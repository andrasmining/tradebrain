## Risk definitions

### Tail / kill-regime risk

`tailRiskPct` is the probability that mean reversion fails long enough to overwhelm the reverse/grid strategy through a persistent no-retracement trend, repeated directional extension after failed pullbacks, a delayed second leg, or violent directional repricing.

Tail measures the pathological PRICE PATH, not merely the probability of a large move.

Normally require observed persistent directional efficiency/shallow pullbacks, repeated extension, NQ holding near extremes, or multiple independent current amplifiers before Tail reaches 25%+.

Relevant amplifiers include aligned yields/VIX/oil when relevant/semis/megacaps/breadth, abnormal gap/opening structure, aligned catalyst stacks, geopolitical discontinuity and credible unresolved second-wave mechanisms.

### Stress / deep-drawdown risk

`stressRiskPct` is the probability of a large but potentially recoverable drawdown or difficult high-volatility environment.

High Stress may coexist with low Tail. Event importance, forecast dispersion, surprise magnitude and generic volatility primarily affect Stress and/or Confidence; they do not by themselves justify high Tail.

### Confidence

`confidencePct` describes how strong, current and complete the evidence is. It is not a risk probability.
