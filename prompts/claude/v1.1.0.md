## Risk definitions

`tailRiskPct` estimates the probability that mean reversion fails for long enough to overwhelm the reverse/mean-reversion/grid strategy through a persistent no-retracement trend, repeated extension after failed pullbacks, a delayed second leg, or violent directional repricing. Tail measures the pathological PRICE PATH, not merely the probability of a large move.

Normally require observed persistent directional efficiency, shallow pullbacks, repeated extension after failed retracements, NQ holding near extremes, or multiple independent current amplifiers before Tail reaches 25%+. Potential amplifiers include aligned yields/VIX/oil/semis/megacaps/breadth, abnormal gap/opening structure, aligned catalyst stacks, geopolitical discontinuity, or a credible unresolved second information wave. Conflicting signals should usually raise Stress more than Tail.

`stressRiskPct` estimates the probability of a large but potentially recoverable basket drawdown or difficult high-volatility environment. High Stress may coexist with low Tail. Event importance, forecast dispersion, surprise magnitude and generic volatility primarily affect Stress and/or Confidence. Stress alone does not switch the strategies off.

`confidencePct` describes the quality, freshness and completeness of the evidence. It is not a risk probability.

## Fixed ranges

Tail: 0-19 `low`; 20-24 `watch`; 25-34 `elevated`; 35-49 `high`; 50-100 `critical`.

Stress: 0-24 `low`; 25-44 `moderate`; 45-64 `elevated`; 65-79 `high`; 80-100 `extreme`.

Confidence: 0-39 `low`; 40-59 `medium-low`; 60-74 `solid`; 75-89 `high`; 90-100 `very-high`.

## Fixed action mapping

Derive CURRENT action from CURRENT Tail only:

- 0-19 → `EA_ON`
- 20-24 → `WATCH`
- 25-34 → `BLOCK_NEW_BASE_ENTRIES`
- 35-49 → `STRONG_BLOCK_NO_NEW_RISK`
- 50-100 → `EA_OFF_NO_NEW_RISK`

Map status exactly: `EA_ON`→`green`; `WATCH`→`yellow`; `BLOCK_NEW_BASE_ENTRIES`→`orange`; `STRONG_BLOCK_NO_NEW_RISK`→`red`; `EA_OFF_NO_NEW_RISK`→`red`.

The same action applies to NQ futures and NAS100 CFD. Existing-position management, exits and emergency protection remain active. Prefer narrow evidence-based danger windows over broad whole-session shutdowns.

U.S. cash open, power hour, NFP, CPI, PPI, PCE, Retail Sales and other high-impact events are checkpoints/catalysts, not automatic Tail blocks. Before an event prints, do not treat hypothetical post-event alignment as already observed. After an event prints, score the observed price path. FOMC may require special care because it is structurally multi-stage.
